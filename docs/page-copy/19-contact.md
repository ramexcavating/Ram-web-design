## Section — Enquiry form

**Heading:** `Send Us a Message`

Fields — keep it short. Every extra field costs you submissions, and a serious buyer will phone anyway.

```
Name *
Company
Phone *
Email *
What is this about? *  (dropdown)
    - Request a quote / estimating enquiry
    - Tender invitation
    - Prequalification package request
    - Existing project enquiry
    - Careers / employment
    - General enquiry
Project location
Tell us about your project *  (long text)
[ Send message ]
```

**Set the form up properly:**

1. **Send to a monitored inbox.** Not a personal address that goes unread on holiday. `[[ DECIDE which inbox ]]`
2. **Put the page name and the subject dropdown value in the email subject line** — e.g. `Website enquiry — Tender invitation — Contact page`. That way you know where an enquiry came from and can triage it at a glance.
3. **Route careers enquiries elsewhere.** Resumes mixed in with tender invitations is how good applicants get lost.
4. **Turn on an autoresponse** confirming you received it and giving the phone number. A buyer who gets silence for two days assumes nobody is there and calls the next contractor.
5. **Enable reCAPTCHA or the builder's spam filter.** A public form on a construction site attracts a lot of junk, and real enquiries get missed in it.
6. **Test it yourself, now and every quarter.** Submit the form and confirm the email arrives. A silently broken contact form is the single most common way a website loses work, and you will not get a complaint — the enquiry just never arrives.

## Structured data (optional, worth doing)

If your builder lets you add custom code to the page head, add this. It helps Google display your business details correctly in search results and reinforces your NAP consistency.

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "GeneralContractor",
  "name": "RAM Excavating Limited",
  "description": "COR-certified civil construction contractor serving British Columbia and Alberta since 1986.",
  "url": "https://ramexcavating.ca",
  "telephone": "+1-250-617-5297",
  "email": "Info@ramexcavating.ca",
  "foundingDate": "1986",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "1280 Quesnel-Hixon Road",
    "addressLocality": "Quesnel",
    "addressRegion": "BC",
    "addressCountry": "CA"
  },
  "areaServed": ["British Columbia", "Alberta", "Saskatchewan"],
  "openingHours": "Mo-Fr 09:00-17:00",
  "sameAs": ["https://www.facebook.com/Ramexcavating"]
}
```

Wrap it in a `<script type="application/ld+json">` tag. Update the address if the canonical one turns out to be Winword Rd, and add your LinkedIn and YouTube URLs to `sameAs` once you have them.

If your builder does not allow custom head code, skip it — your Google Business Profile does most of the same job, and that matters more.
