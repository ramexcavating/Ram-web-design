## Intro

```
RAM Excavating covers the full scope of civil and heavy construction — from
clearing raw ground through to commissioning finished infrastructure. We
self-perform the majority of our work with our own crews and our own
equipment, which keeps schedule, cost and quality under our direct control
rather than subcontracted out.

Every service below is delivered under the same COR-certified safety program
and the same quality control standards, whether the client is a municipality,
a mine, an industrial plant owner or a general contractor.
```

## Service listing

Ten entries. Each is a heading, two lines, and a link. Keep them in this order — it runs roughly in the sequence work happens on a site, which reads naturally.

### Land Clearing
```
Right-of-way and site clearing, grubbing, mulching and debris management for
mine, pipeline, road and development projects. The first crew on site and
often the one that sets the schedule for everything after.
```
`Land clearing →` `/services/land-clearing`

### Earthworks & Site Preparation
```
Bulk excavation, mass grading, structural fill placement and building pad
construction. Cut and fill balanced on site wherever the material allows,
which keeps haul costs and truck traffic down.
```
`Earthworks and site preparation →` `/services/earthworks-site-preparation`

### Civil Construction
```
Our core discipline. Watermains, sanitary and storm sewer, street
reconstruction and underground utilities for municipalities across western
Canada — thirty years of it.
```
`Civil construction →` `/services/civil-construction`

### Water & Sewer Installation
```
New installation and replacement of water, sanitary and storm systems,
including deep mains, high water table conditions and live-service tie-ins
under traffic.
```
`Water and sewer installation →` `/services/water-sewer-installation`

### Auger Boring & Trenchless
```
Trenchless crossings under highways, rail and watercourses. No open cut, no
lane closure, no watercourse disturbance — and no permit fight over either.
```
`Auger boring and trenchless →` `/services/auger-boring-trenchless`

### HDPE Pipe Fusion
```
Certified butt fusion and electrofusion of HDPE pipe for water, sewer, mine

```
`HDPE pipe fusion →` `/services/hdpe-pipe-fusion`

### Dewatering & Environmental Solutions

```
Construction dewatering, sediment and erosion control, water treatment and
```

`Dewatering and environmental →` `/services/dewatering-environmental`

### Road Building & Highway Construction

## Section — General contracting and construction management

**Heading:** `General Contracting, Design-Build and Construction Management`

```
Beyond self-performed civil work, RAM takes on projects as general contractor,
under design-build, and on a construction management basis. On those projects
we carry the schedule, the subtrades, the permits and the client relationship
— and we still self-perform the civil scope ourselves, which removes the
usual gap between the party holding the schedule and the party doing the
earthworks.

If you are weighing up delivery models for a project, call us early. It is
a cheaper conversation before the design is finished than after.
```
