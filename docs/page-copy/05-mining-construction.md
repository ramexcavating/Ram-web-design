## Section — What we build

**Heading:** `Mine Site Civil Scope`

- **Haul roads** — construction, widening and upgrade to mine haul standards, built for loaded truck traffic and maintainable through a wet season
- **Underground portal construction** — portal pad, cut, collar and supporting civil works
- **Settling and polishing ponds** — excavation, lining preparation, berms, inlet and outlet structures, spillways
- **Dump site management** — waste rock and overburden dump construction, lift management, access and stability considerations
- **Exploration support** — drill pad construction, access trail and road building, reclamation of completed pads
- **Site access roads** — main access construction and upgrade, including watercourse crossings
- **Site water management** — ditching, diversion, sediment control ponds and dewatering
- **Pads and platforms** — crusher, conveyor, plant, camp, laydown and fuel facility pads
- **Site services** — potable water, process water, fire protection loops, sanitary systems and mine dewatering pipe
- **Reclamation earthworks** — recontouring, cover placement and revegetation preparation

## Section — Working on an operating mine

**Heading:** `Working Around Live Mine Operations`

```
Building on a greenfield site and building on a producing mine are different
jobs. On an operating site, production comes first, your crew is a guest in
someone else's traffic pattern, and the safety expectations are set by the
mine, not by the contractor.

We work to it:

- Site-specific orientation and mine-specific inductions completed before anyone starts
- Our crews integrate with the mine's traffic management, radio protocol and right-of-way rules
- Work planned around production windows and shutdown schedules rather than against them
- Daily field level hazard assessments referencing the mine's hazards, not a generic template
- Incident reporting into the mine's system as well as our own
- Prequalification and compliance documentation kept current so we clear the gate rather than delaying mobilisation
```

That last point is worth stating plainly on the page. Mines lose weeks to contractors who cannot get through the compliance gate, and a contractor who volunteers that they keep their documentation current is answering a question the contracts manager was about to ask.
