**Heading:** `About RAM Excavating Limited`

```
RAM Excavating was founded in 1986 by Raymond Alexander Mickey and remains
a family-run business today. What started as a single-machine excavating
operation in the Cariboo now works right across western Canada from offices
in Quesnel, BC and Grande Cache, Alberta.

We have worked in municipalities across western Canada constructing and
replacing watermains, sanitary and storm sewer mains, reconstructing streets
and installing underground utilities. Alongside that municipal work we build
new mine sites, upgrade industrial plant infrastructure, and construct public
highway to BC MOTI standards.

In recent years RAM has grown substantially in equipment fleet, workforce and
```

## Section — Management Systems

**Heading:** `How We Run a Project`

**Intro:**

```
Three systems sit behind every RAM project. They are the reason our clients
get the schedule, the quality and the safety record they were promised.
```

### Card 1 — Project Management System

```
Every RAM project runs under a single accountable project manager from award
to close-out. Schedules are built and maintained in Microsoft Project,
progress is tracked against the baseline, and changes, RFIs and submittals
are logged and answered rather than left to accumulate. Owners and
consultants get a straight answer on where the work stands, when they
ask for it.
```

### Card 2 — Quality Control System

```
Our quality control program is built around BC MOTI standards — some of the
most demanding in the country — and applies to every project regardless of
client. Materials are verified against specification on delivery, compaction
and grade are tested and documented as the work proceeds, and deficiencies
are recorded and closed before we hand over. The result is infrastructure
that performs the way the engineer designed it to.
```

This replaces your current Quality Control text, which repeats the Mining Construction description and cuts off mid-sentence at "preferred choice f".

### Card 3 — Safety Program

```
RAM is COR® certified through the BC Construction Safety Alliance. Our
safety program meets provincial and federal regulation and is implemented
on every project — not filed and forgotten. Daily field level hazard
assessments, documented site orientations, toolbox meetings and formal
incident investigation are standard on all of our work. The safety of our
people is the first consideration on every job we take.
```
