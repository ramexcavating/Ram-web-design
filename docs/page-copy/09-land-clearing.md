## Section — What we do

**Heading:** `Land Clearing Scope`

- **Site clearing** — full clearing of development, industrial, mine and municipal sites
- **Right-of-way clearing** — road, highway, pipeline, transmission line and utility corridors
- **Grubbing and stripping** — stump and root removal, organics stripping, topsoil salvage and stockpiling
- **Mulching and mastication** — mechanical mulching where debris is best left on site as ground cover
- **Merchantable timber handling** — decking and sorting for salvage where there is a value recovery, rather than burying it
- **Debris management** — burning under permit, chipping, grinding, hauling and disposal
- **Slash and windrow management**
- **Selective and partial clearing** — where retention areas, buffers or riparian setbacks have to be preserved exactly
- **Access trail and drill pad clearing** — exploration support in remote terrain
- **Erosion and sediment control** — installed as clearing proceeds, not after the first rain event

## Section — Permits, timing windows and retention

**Heading:** `Clearing Inside the Rules`

```
Clearing is one of the most heavily conditioned activities on a project.
Migratory bird nesting windows, fisheries-sensitive timing around watercourses,
riparian setbacks, burning permits and archaeological or cultural heritage
constraints all apply — and a contractor who clears through one of them turns
a clearing item into a regulatory problem for the owner.

We plan the work around those conditions:

- Timing windows identified and scheduled around before mobilisation
- Retention areas, buffers and riparian setbacks flagged on the ground and respected
- Burning done under valid permit and inside venting conditions
- Erosion and sediment control installed progressively as ground is exposed
- Watercourse and wetland setbacks maintained, with crossings only where permitted
- Cultural and archaeological constraints handled as directed by the owner and any monitors on site

If your clearing window is tight, tell us early. A clearing schedule that has
to fit around a bird window is a very different plan from one that does not,
and it is not something to work out after award.
```

This section is a genuine differentiator. Almost no clearing contractor's website mentions timing windows, and it is the first thing an experienced project manager or environmental consultant worries about.
