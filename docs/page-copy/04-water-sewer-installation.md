## Section — Watermain

**Heading:** `Watermain Construction and Replacement`

- New watermain installation in PVC, ductile iron and HDPE across the full range of municipal diameters
- Full replacement of aging or undersized main, including asbestos cement main removal under proper controls
- Valves, hydrants, air valves, blow-offs, chambers and appurtenances
- Service connections and reconnections, including curb stops and meter setting
- Live tie-ins to existing systems, staged to minimise or eliminate customer shutdowns
- Pressure testing, disinfection, flushing and bacteriological clearance to owner specification
- Thrust blocking, restrained joints and proper bedding — the details that decide whether a main lasts

## Section — Difficult conditions

**Heading:** `Deep, Wet and Difficult Installations`

```
The easy pipe is not what a contractor gets judged on. These are the
conditions we are set up for:

**Deep excavation.** Engineered shoring and trench shielding for installations
well below the depths a standard trench box handles. Our crews work to a
documented excavation and shoring procedure under our COR-certified program,
and nobody enters an unprotected trench.

**High water table.** Sump and wellpoint dewatering to get and keep the trench
workable, with discharge managed inside permit conditions rather than pumped
straight to the nearest ditch. See our dewatering page for how we handle
sediment and discharge compliance.

**Rock.** Rock excavation, trimming and bedding correction so the pipe sits on
proper bedding and not on point loads.

**Unstable and contaminated ground.** Over-excavation and structural
replacement where native material won't support the pipe, and segregated
handling and disposal where material is contaminated.

**Live services.** Locating, hand-exposing and protecting existing utilities
before machine excavation anywhere near them.
```

## Section — Why RAM

**Heading:** `Why This Work Suits Us`

```
Water and sewer is buried work, which means the owner is trusting the
contractor about things nobody can see once the trench is closed. Bedding.
Compaction. Whether the joint was made properly. Whether the main was
actually disinfected.

We document as we go — compaction testing, grade, pressure and bacteriological
results — so the owner has a record rather than an assurance. That paperwork
is also what makes the difference at the end of a job, when a question comes
up about a section installed three months earlier.

Our crews include certified HDPE fusion technicians, so we make our own HDPE
joints rather than subcontracting them and hoping.
```
