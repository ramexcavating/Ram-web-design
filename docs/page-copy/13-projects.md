## How to write each entry

Six fields, every time. Consistency is what makes the page scannable.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Project name** | The name the client used. If it's confidential, use scope and place instead: "Watermain Replacement, Quesnel BC" |
| **Client / client type** | Name them if you have permission. If not, "Municipal client" or "BC-based mining operator" is fine and still counts |
| **Location** | Town and province. **Never skip this** — it's what makes the page rank |
| **Year** | Year completed. Recent work carries more weight, so lead with it |
| **Scope** | 3–5 bullets of what you actually did, with quantities where you have them. Quantities are the single most persuasive thing on the page |
| **Value** | Approximate contract value, or a band: under $500K / $500K–$2M / $2M–$5M / $5M+. A band is fine if you'd rather not publish exact figures |

**On quantities:** "1,850 m of 300 mm PVC watermain" tells an estimator far more than "watermain replacement". If you have the numbers, use them. They are the difference between a page that reads as proof and a page that reads as marketing.

> throughout.
>
> - 1,850 m of 300 mm PVC watermain, including 22 service reconnections
> - 940 m of 250 mm sanitary sewer at depths to 4.8 m with trench shoring
> - Live-service tie-ins completed overnight to avoid daytime shutdowns
> - Full street reconstruction, curb, gutter and sidewalk reinstatement
> - Completed on schedule with no lost-time incidents

Note what that entry does: it names a quantity, it names a constraint (keeping businesses open), and it closes on safety. Those three things are what a municipal engineer is actually reading for.

## Images

**This page must have photos.** A projects page without them is not persuasive, and photos are the whole reason a buyer opens it.

- 2–3 photos per project, minimum 1
- Real photos of your own work — never stock
- Crews in full PPE. A photo with a worker not wearing a hard hat undermines the entire Safety page, and mine and industrial buyers do notice
- Include at least one open-trench-with-shoring shot. It reads as competence to anyone technical
- Compress before uploading — a page with 30 full-size photos loads slowly on a phone, which is where site staff will open it

**Alt text** for each: `[Scope] completed by RAM Excavating on the [project name] project in [town], [province]`. Write each one out — this is free SEO and most contractors never bother.
