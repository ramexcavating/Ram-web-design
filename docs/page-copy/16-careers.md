## Section — Why work here

**Heading:** `Why RAM`

```
**Steady work.** We run a mix of municipal, mine, industrial and highway work
across three provinces, which means the season is longer and the slow periods
are shorter than at outfits that depend on one client or one sector.

**Safety that is real.** We are COR® certified through the BC Construction
Safety Alliance. That means audited, not claimed. Our crews have the authority
to stop work they believe is unsafe, without needing permission and without
consequence.

**Good equipment.** We have invested heavily in the fleet in recent years. You
will not spend your shift fighting a machine that should have been replaced
three years ago.

**Family owned.** The people who own this company are on the sites. Decisions
get made by someone you can actually talk to, and there is no head office in
another province overruling your foreman.

**Training and tickets.** We train our people and we pay for the tickets. If
you want to move from labourer to operator, or operator to foreman, tell us —
we would rather promote from inside.

**Work that lasts.** The water and sewer under these streets, the highways
between these towns, the mine sites that keep the region working. Nearly forty
years of it. It is worth something to drive past a job and know you built it.
```

## Section — What we look for

**Heading:** `What We Look For`

```
Experience matters and tickets matter, but the two things that decide whether
somebody lasts here are simpler than that.

**You look out for the people around you.** Ground workers near mobile
equipment, a trench somebody is about to enter, a young guy who does not know
what he does not know yet. If you see it and say something, you will fit in
here.

**You show up.** Civil construction runs on crews that are there at the start
of shift, ready to work. Everything else can be taught.
```
