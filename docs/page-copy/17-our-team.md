This is the public-facing team page. Its job is to turn a company into people. Engineers and development managers award work partly on who they will be dealing with, and a named project manager with twenty years in the Cariboo is more reassuring than any paragraph about commitment to excellence.

It also gives a procurement officer the direct route to the right person, which is worth more than a general enquiry form.

## Images needed

One photo per person, plus a crew shot.

**On the photos.** Consistency matters more than production value here — six photos taken the same way look deliberate, while a mix of studio headshots, cropped wedding photos and Facebook profile pictures looks careless. The cheapest way to get this right is to take them all yourself on the same afternoon, outdoors, in RAM branded gear or PPE, with the same background. That reads better for a construction company than formal studio portraits would anyway.

**Alt text** for each: `[Name], [Title] at RAM Excavating Limited`.

---

## One thing to decide before you build this page

Some of your people may not want a photo and a name on a public website, and that is a reasonable position. Ask first rather than assuming.

If someone declines, use their name and role without a photo, or list the role without the name — a "Senior Project Manager, 22 years with RAM" entry with no photo still does most of the credibility work. What you should not do is quietly leave a key contact off the page entirely, because a buyer looking for a project manager needs to find one.
