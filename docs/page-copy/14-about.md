## Section — History

**Heading:** `Our History`

```
RAM Excavating was founded in 1986 by Raymond Alexander Mickey. What began as
a single-machine excavating operation working locally in the Cariboo has grown
into a civil contractor operating across three provinces from offices in
Quesnel, British Columbia and Grande Cache, Alberta.

The company is still owned and run by the Mickey family. That continuity is
worth something practical to a client: the person who commits to your schedule
is the person who has to live with it, and there is no head office in another
province overruling a decision made on your site.

Over the past thirty years we have worked in municipalities across western
Canada constructing and replacing watermains, sanitary and storm sewer mains,
reconstructing streets and installing underground utilities. Alongside that
municipal work we have built new mine sites, upgraded industrial plant
infrastructure, and constructed public highway to BC Ministry of Transportation
and Infrastructure standards.

In recent years RAM has grown substantially in equipment fleet, workforce and
financial capacity — which means we take on larger and more complex projects
than at any point in our history.
```

## Section — How we work

**Heading:** `How We Run a Project`

```
Three systems sit behind every RAM project.

**Project management.** Every project runs under a single accountable project
manager from award to close-out. Schedules are built and maintained properly,
progress is tracked against baseline, and changes, RFIs and submittals are
logged and answered rather than left to accumulate. When an owner or consultant
asks where the work stands, they get a straight answer.

**Quality control.** Our quality control program is built around BC MOTI
standards and applies to every project regardless of client. Materials verified
against specification on delivery, compaction and grade tested and documented as
the work proceeds, deficiencies recorded and closed before handover rather than
negotiated afterwards.

**Safety.** RAM is COR® certified through the BC Construction Safety Alliance.
Our program meets provincial and federal regulation and is implemented on every
project — daily field level hazard assessments, documented site orientations,
toolbox meetings and formal incident investigation.
```
